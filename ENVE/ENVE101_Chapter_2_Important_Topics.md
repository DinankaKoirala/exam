## A — Highest Priority

### 1. Hydrologic Cycle and Hydrologic Processes
Know the water cycle and definitions of:
- precipitation;
- evaporation;
- transpiration;
- infiltration;
- runoff;
- subsurface flow.
Also know **catchment / drainage basin / watershed** and watershed divide.
- **PDF:** pp. 2–5.

### 2. Water-Balance Equation
Core relation in the lecture:
- `ΔS = P - Q - E`
More general worked-example form:
- `ΔS = P + R_in - R_out - ET`

Be able to convert rainfall/runoff depth to volume using catchment area.
- **PDF:** pp. 6–9.

### 3. Water-Supply System
- Definition and objectives.
- Surface vs groundwater sources.
- Potable vs polluted water.
- Main components: **intake → transmission → treatment → distribution**.
- Urban/rural system schematics.
- **PDF:** pp. 12–21.

### 4. Design Period, Population Forecast and Water Demand
Know:
- arithmetic increase method: `P_n = P + nI`
- geometric increase method: `P_n = P(1 + i/100)^n`
- converting projected population × per-capita demand into total demand.
- **PDF:** pp. 22–28.

### 5. Sewerage Terminology and Components
Definitions:
- sewage, sullage, sewer, sewerage;
- DWF and WWF;
- black water, grey water, night soil/fecal sludge.

Sewer components/order:
- house sewer;
- lateral sewer;
- branch/sub-main sewer;
- main/trunk sewer;
- outfall sewer.
- **PDF:** pp. 29–38.

### 6. Combined vs Separate Sewer Systems
Know:
- operation of each system;
- comparison table: rainfall suitability, street width, pollution, sewer size, treatment and maintenance cost.
- **PDF:** pp. 39–41.

### 7. Sewer Design Considerations and Formulas
High-value numerical section:
- wastewater return relation used in lecture: `Q_ww = 0.8 Q_ws`
- sewers are not designed to run full;
- design period;
- peak factor and maximum flow;
- `Maximum flow = Peak factor × Average flow`
- `Minimum flow = (1/3) × Average flow`
- storm runoff:
  - `Q = CIA/360`
  - `Q` in `m³/s`, `I` in `mm/hr`, `A` in `ha`
- rainfall intensity:
  - for `5–20 min`: `I = 760/(t + 10)`
  - for `20–100 min`: `I = 1020/(t + 20)`
- **PDF:** pp. 42–48.

### 8. Water-Quality Parameters
Know the three categories and examples.

**Physical**
- solids: suspended, colloidal, dissolved;
- turbidity;
- color;
- taste and odor.

**Chemical**
- alkalinity;
- hardness;
- fluoride;
- metals;
- biodegradable organics;
- nutrients: nitrogen and phosphorus.

**Biological**
- pathogens;
- E. coli and total coliform as indicators.
- **PDF:** pp. 49–61.

### 9. Water-Treatment Process
Know the purpose and the major units, principle and objective:
1. Screening
2. Aeration
3. Sedimentation/coagulation
4. Filtration
5. Disinfection
- **PDF:** pp. 62–64.

### 10. Wastewater-Treatment Basics
Know the three categories:
- **Physical:** screens, grit chamber, skimming, sedimentation.
- **Chemical:** coagulation/precipitation.
- **Biological:** microorganisms consume biodegradable organics and produce biomass/biological floc.
- **PDF:** pp. 65–68.
