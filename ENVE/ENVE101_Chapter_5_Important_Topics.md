## A — Highest Priority

### 1. Waste, Solid Waste and Solid-Waste Management
Know the definitions of:
- waste;
- solid waste;
- solid-waste management.
Know the controlled elements:
- generation;
- storage;
- collection;
- transfer/transport;
- processing;
- disposal.
- **PDF:** pp. 2–3.

### 2. Integrated Solid-Waste Management (ISWM) and Hierarchy
Know the concept of integrated management and the hierarchy shown:
1. Source reduction & reuse
2. Recycling
3. Composting
4. Waste-to-energy
5. Landfill
- **PDF:** pp. 4–5.

### 3. Functional Elements
Definitions of:
- source/generation;
- collection;
- transfer and transport;
- processing and recovery;
- disposal.
- **PDF:** pp. 7–8.

### 4. Classification of Solid Waste
Know classification:
- by source/generator;
- by component;
- by regulation: hazardous vs non-hazardous.

Specific categories:
- municipal/residential;
- industrial;
- agricultural;
- medical/clinical/non-clinical;
- electronic waste.
- **PDF:** pp. 9–17.

### 5. Hazardous Waste
Know the definition and source/property examples:
- explosivity;
- corrosivity;
- inflammability;
- toxicity.
- **PDF:** pp. 14–17.

### 6. Properties of Municipal Solid Waste
Three groups:
- **Physical:** particle size, moisture content, density, field capacity, permeability.
- **Chemical:** proximate analysis, ash-fusion point, energy content, ultimate analysis, water-soluble constituents.
- **Biological:** cellulose-related components and assimilation-capacity concept.
- **PDF:** pp. 18–21.

### 7. Important Formulas
**Moisture content**
- `MC(%) = (Wet mass - Dry mass) / Wet mass × 100`

**Modified Dulong formula**
- `Heat Value (kJ/kg) = 337C + 1428(H - O/8) + 95S`
- `C, H, O, S` are the percentages used in the lecture formula.
- **PDF:** pp. 19–20.

### 8. Collection Systems and Transfer Stations
Collection systems:
- door-to-door;
- alley;
- set-out;
- set-out and set-back/backyard.

Transfer station:
- purpose;
- transfer from small collection vehicles to larger long-distance vehicles;
- route diagram.
- **PDF:** pp. 24–29.

### 9. Incineration
Know:
- definition/process;
- where it is used;
- need to separate combustible/non-combustible waste;
- basic incinerator flow diagram.
- **PDF:** pp. 33–34.

### 10. Sanitary Landfill, Leachate and Post-Closure Care
Know:
- sanitary-landfill concept;
- compaction and soil cover;
- containment/leachate control;
- leachate definition and water-balance concept;
- post-closure monitoring and maintenance.
- **PDF:** pp. 35–41.

### 11. Landfill vs Incinerator
Memorize the comparison dimensions:
- initial cost;
- operating cost;
- land requirement;
- fixed vs changeable location;
- final disposal/residue.
- **PDF:** p. 39.
