1. Carbon monoxide (`CO`)
2. Lead (`Pb`)
3. Nitrogen oxides (`NOx`)

5. Particulate matter (`PM`)
6. Sulfur oxides (`SOx`)

### 4. Major Pollutant Details
High-value short-answer material:

- **Pb:** major sources/effects in the lecture.

### 6. Air-Pollution Control Strategies
Three strategies in the lecture:
1. Reduce pollution at source through material/process changes.
2. Use pollution-control equipment at source.
3. Dilution/dispersion using a tall stack.
- **PDF:** p. 29.

### 7. Cyclone Separator
Know:

- schematic and particle path.
- **PDF:** pp. 30–31.

### 8. Electrostatic Precipitator (ESP)
Know:
- purpose and industrial use;
- negative wire electrodes and positive collector plates;
- particle ionization and collection mechanism;
- schematic.
- **PDF:** pp. 32–34.

### 9. Deutsch–Anderson Equation for ESP
Must know:
- `η = 1 - exp(-ωA/Q)`
- `A = -(Q/ω) ln(1 - η)`

Where:
- `η` = fractional collection efficiency;
- `A` = collector-plate area;
- `Q` = gas volumetric flow rate;
- `ω` = effective drift velocity.
- **PDF:** pp. 35–36.
